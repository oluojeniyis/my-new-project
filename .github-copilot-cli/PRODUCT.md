# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Retail buyers shopping for individual items such as polos and accessories.
- Wholesale buyers purchasing blank apparel in bulk, subject to minimum quantities.
- Custom clients requesting bespoke or custom mesh clothing and sharing design notes.

## Product Purpose

TeesTale is an online shop for wholesale and retail apparel and custom mesh clothing. Success means customers can find suitable products, configure items, add them to a cart, and move through checkout.

## Positioning

One shop brings wholesale blanks, retail polos, bespoke custom mesh, and accessories together for customers with different apparel needs.

## Operating Context

Customers browse the shop, filter by category, inspect product details, choose quantity, size, and color, and proceed through the cart and checkout. Wholesale products have a minimum quantity. Custom clients may need to provide design notes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The requested Phase 2 scope is a Next.js App Router and Tailwind CSS interface with seeded product examples across all four categories, category filters, product details, cart interactions, and a checkout page layout. Auth, database, email, payment, and other external integrations are out of scope for this phase.

The PRD names retail and wholesale price fields but provides no catalog prices, currency, or concrete product catalog. Those values remain undecided; do not present invented prices as real. Seeded names, descriptions, sizes, and colors are illustrative until confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is TeesTale. Its catalog spans wholesale blanks, retail polos, bespoke/custom mesh, and accessories.

## Evidence on Hand

The user-provided PRD is the source for product categories, user types, and requested flows. No existing brand assets, product imagery, or real catalog data were present in the workspace.

## Product Principles

- Keep the four product categories discoverable in one shop.
- Make product configuration clear before adding an item to the cart.
- Enforce wholesale minimum quantities in the shopping flow.
- Give custom clients a place to describe their design needs.
